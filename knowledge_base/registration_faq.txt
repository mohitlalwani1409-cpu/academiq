================================================================================
COURSE REGISTRATION FAQ & POLICIES
================================================================================

1. REGISTRATION DEADLINES AND DATES
Course registration opens in April for the Fall semester and November for the Spring semester. Exact registration windows are determined by student classification:
- Seniors (90+ credits): Day 1
- Juniors (60-89 credits): Day 3
- Sophomores (30-59 credits): Day 5
- Freshmen (0-29 credits): Day 7

2. ADD/DROP AND WITHDRAWAL PERIODS
- Add/Drop Week: The first 7 calendar days of the semester. Students can add or drop courses without penalty or record on their transcript. Dropped classes are fully refunded.
- Course Withdrawal: From week 2 through week 10, students can withdraw from a course. A grade of 'W' will be recorded on their transcript. The 'W' does not affect GPA, but tuition is not refunded. No withdrawals are allowed after week 10.

3. CREDIT LOAD LIMITS
- Standard Load: The typical course load is 15-16 credit hours per semester.
- Maximum Load: The maximum allowed is 18 credit hours.
- Overload Exception: Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.2 or higher may request a credit overload (up to 21 credits) by submitting a request form signed by their department head and academic advisor.

4. PREREQUISITE WAIVERS
Students must complete all listed course prerequisites before registering. If a student has equivalent experience, they may request a prerequisite waiver from the course instructor. Once approved, the department will issue an electronic permit allowing registration.
