================================================================================
GRADING AND GPA POLICIES
================================================================================

1. GPA CALCULATION METHOD (How to calculate GPA)
The Grade Point Average (GPA) is calculated by dividing the total number of grade points earned by the total number of credit hours attempted. Grade points are assigned as follows:
- A  = 4.0 points per credit hour
- B  = 3.0 points per credit hour
- C  = 2.0 points per credit hour
- D  = 1.0 points per credit hour
- F  = 0.0 points per credit hour
Pass/Fail (P/F) courses and withdrawn courses (W) do not impact the GPA calculation.

2. ACADEMIC PROBATION
Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher.
- Academic Warning: Triggered if a student's GPA falls below 2.0 in a single semester.
- Academic Probation: If a student's cumulative GPA falls below 2.0, they are placed on Academic Probation. They must meet with an advisor to set up an Academic Success Plan and are limited to a maximum of 12 credits per semester.
- Academic Suspension: If a student on probation fails to raise their cumulative GPA to 2.0 or achieve a term GPA of 2.5 in the subsequent semester, they face suspension.

3. REPEATING COURSES AND GRADE FORGIVENESS
Students can repeat a course in which they received a grade of D or F. Under the Grade Forgiveness Policy:
- A student may repeat up to three (3) courses to replace the original grades.
- The new grade, whether higher or lower, will be used to calculate the cumulative GPA.
- The original grade remains on the transcript but is excluded from the GPA calculation.

4. DEAN'S LIST REQUIREMENTS
Full-time students (attempting 12 or more graded credits) who achieve a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher with no grades of F or Incomplete are placed on the Dean's List.
